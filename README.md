# Jens Almanac/POLTERWORX
A taken down mod for Balatro, brought back because mods shouldn't be held at the hands of indie mods who are wanna-be corporations.
## What?
Jens Almanac, or, as I will refer to it from here on out, POLTERWORX, was a mod created by Jen Walter that was taken down on June 23rd, 2025, at 5:09 PM EDT, citing backlash from the community for stolen assets that he had since apologized and replaced, and other community mishaps that he was not able to handle as a developer, requesting that everyone who has a copy of POLTERWORX not to distribute it, but said that other mods that he had created (Incantation, JenLib, Aurinko, and Nopeus) were okay to redistribute. Originally, all of the listed mods, minus JenLib and POLTERWORX itself had GitHub repos, but all were taken down, including the version of Cryptid that POLTERWORX expects. All of which have been uploaded here.
## Why?
POLTERWORX had a lot of potential as a Balatro mod, adding onto Cryptid, and had a community, even after it's takedown, on a Garry's Mod server with a plugin Jen had made called "nZombies". However, recently, the Discord server itself was taken down for an unknown reason, therefore, there is pretty much **no way to recover** any messages sent within the community, disintegrating it almost entirely. Taking down a Balatro mod because of community backlash, rather than doing something reasonable, such as passing the torch of development down onto *very willing* mod developers, or simply stepping away from development and leaving the community, preserving the community by itself, is an act that a corporation would take because a product is not making enough money.
Jen Walter is someone who not only abandoned their community, but took it down with them, an indie modder acting like a video game publisher, and could not take flak for his own actions and being held accountable. He did, for a short while, before tearing it all apart at his own hands.
